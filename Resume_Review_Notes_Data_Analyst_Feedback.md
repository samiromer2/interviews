# Resume Review Notes -- Data Analyst Feedback

These are the recommendations I received from a Data Analyst after
reviewing my resume.

## Overall

-   Target **Entry-Level Data Analyst** roles.
-   Keep the resume to **1--1.5 pages** (ideally one page).
-   Make sure the resume clearly shows that **you have what is needed
    for a first Data Analyst role**.
-   If there are employment gaps, be prepared to explain them during
    interviews in a simple and honest way. Be human.

## Resume Order

1.  Summary of Qualifications
2.  Education
3.  Projects
4.  Professional Experience (including Volunteer Experience)
5.  Technical Skills

## Summary of Qualifications

-   Maximum **5 bullet points**.
-   Focus on:
    -   Relevant experience
    -   Education
    -   Skills related to the target role
    -   Python
    -   SQL
-   Remove anything that isn't directly relevant.

## Resume Title

-   Change the headline from **Junior Data Analyst** to something like:
    -   **AI & Data Analytics (NPower)**
    -   Or another title that reflects your current training until you
        complete the program.
-   Make sure your LinkedIn headline matches.

## Projects

-   Include only **1--2 projects**.
-   Make them as strong as possible.
-   Real-world experience is more valuable than having many projects.
-   Each project should have **3 bullet points maximum**.
-   If needed, revive and improve an older project instead of adding a
    new weak one.

## Professional Experience

-   Volunteer work should **not** have its own section.
-   Move your volunteer work into **Professional Experience**.
-   Use **Volunteer** as the job title if appropriate.
-   Include only **one volunteer experience**, not multiple volunteer
    entries.
-   Keep each experience concise (around **3 bullet points** for
    data-related roles).

## Technical Skills

-   Focus on the essentials.
-   Python and SQL should be emphasized.
-   Remove technologies that aren't helping you get a Data Analyst
    position.
-   Soft skills are fine to include if they're relevant.

## General Advice

-   Employers care more about **1--2 excellent projects** than many
    average ones.
-   Tailor everything toward your first Data Analyst job.
-   Keep the resume focused, concise, and easy to read.
